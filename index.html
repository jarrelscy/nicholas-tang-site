About

The surgeon behind the work

Dr. Nicholas Tang trained at some of the country's leading academic centers, completing a residency in plastic and reconstructive surgery followed by advanced fellowship training in aesthetic and microsurgical technique.

His practice spans the full breadth of plastic surgery — from complex reconstruction after cancer and trauma to refined aesthetic procedures of the face, breast, and body. What unites the work is a single principle: the best surgery is the surgery no one notices.

Every consultation begins with listening. Recommendations are made only when they serve you — and sometimes the right recommendation is to do nothing at all.

Procedures

Focused areas of practice

01

Facial Surgery

Rhinoplasty, facelift, blepharoplasty, and neck contouring — planned around your anatomy, not a trend.

02

Breast Surgery

Augmentation, reduction, lifting, and reconstructive oncologic work with an emphasis on proportion and symmetry.

Body Contouring

Abdominoplasty, liposuction, and post-weight-loss reconstruction that restore shape while respecting natural lines.

Careful correction of prior surgical outcomes, approached with realism about what revision can achieve.
